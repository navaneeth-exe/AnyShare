Analysis of Distributed Systems in Modern Cloud Infrastructure

The proliferation of microservice architectures across enterprise environments has fundamentally restructured how organisations approach fault tolerance and horizontal scalability. The CAP theorem, first articulated by Brewer (2000), remains the canonical framework through which engineers evaluate trade-offs between consistency, availability, and partition tolerance...

This paper examines three primary deployment patterns: (1) service mesh orchestration via Istio, (2) event-driven architectures leveraging Apache Kafka, and (3) the emerging paradigm of edge-native compute.
